About My Journey

Nilesh Vadgave is a PhD student at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, specializing in Technology Development and social entrepreneurship. With a background in Mechanical Engineering and a Post Graduation in Technology & Development, his work focuses on designing modular storage solutions to mitigate agricultural value chain losses. As a transdisciplinary researcher, he integrates technical design with community participation to achieve 'end-to-end innovation'. Nilesh is also the founder of 'O-Zone LLP' and 'CircularKheti', ventures dedicated to climate change and food security. Beyond academia and business, he is a passionate artist involved in theatre, poetry, and youth mentorship.

Academic & Research

Climate Agnostic Storage for Onion Preservation

In India, onion storage losses range from 40-60% in traditional structures. At CTARA, IIT Bombay, we developed 'Climate Agnostic' structures that circulate conditioned air to maintain quality irrespective of external weather. This scientific approach reduces rotting and sprouting, ensuring a stable supply chain for one of India's most vital crops.
